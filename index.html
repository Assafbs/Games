Sugar Push

A west coast swing rhythm game. You lead, the band plays, the slot is yours.

  • Step the rhythm. Left foot is F, right foot is J, or tap the two pads. Feet always alternate: walk, walk, tri‑ple‑step, tri‑ple‑step.
  • Lead the dance. Tap a pattern card (or press 1–6) to choose your next move. Fresh patterns build Flow and multiply your score. Choose nothing and you’ll sugar push all night.
  • Hit the breaks. When the band stops and slams back in, the ★ gold step is worth double.
